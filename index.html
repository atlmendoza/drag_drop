📘 Definitions

Strong “whole–part” relationship; parts die with the whole.
Weak “whole–part” relationship; parts can exist independently.
“Is‑a” relationship; subclass inherits attributes and methods.
Temporary “uses‑a” relationship; one class depends on another.
